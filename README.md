# 🍳 Cook-Book App

🌟 **Excited to Announce the Completion of My Summer Training at Information Technology Institute (ITI)** 🌟

I'm thrilled to share the launch of the **Cook-Book App**, a project I've been working on with my amazing team during our summer training at ITI.

The app allows users to explore a variety of recipes, check ingredients, watch video tutorials, rate their favourite dishes, update their email and password, and even share recipes with friends!

---

## 💡 Key Features

- 🔍 Discover and explore recipes from different cuisines  
- 📋 View meal ingredients and preparation instructions  
- 🎥 Watch video tutorials for step-by-step cooking  
- ⭐ Save and rate your favourite meals  
- 📤 Share recipes easily with friends  
- 🛠️ Update email and password securely  

---

## 📲 Technologies Used

- **Kotlin**  
- **Retrofit**  
- **Room Database**  
- **Motion Layout**  
- **YouTube Player API**
